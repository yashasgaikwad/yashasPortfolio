From Cafe Kitchen to My Own Kitchen —

This is GRILLED AFFAIRS ❤️

“Hi, I'm Chef Riddhi Patil.

For years, I poured my heart into cafe & restaurant kitchens, making everyone else's dream taste delicious. But the 9-to-6 grind, the long shifts away from home... it started getting too difficult.

So I decided — why not build my own dream with my own skills? And that's how Grilled Affairs — by Riddhi & Tushar Patil was born!

A small cloud kitchen from my home in Jogeshwari, serving food made with real chef skills & lots of love.”

— Chef Riddhi & Tushar Patil
